Privacy Policy

Billy is a reminders and bills app made by Kiran Chakravarthi Gunisetti. In short: Billy has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you put in Billy stays on your phone, and we never receive it.

What Billy keeps, and where

Everything is stored only on your device, inside the app:

  • Your tasks and bills: titles, notes, amounts, due dates, repeat settings, groups and whether they’re done or paid.
  • Payment details you type or scan so you know where to pay: for example BPAY biller code and reference, PayID, BSB and account number, or UPI ID, plus records of what you marked as paid.
  • Photos and PDFs you attach or scan.
  • Your settings: country and reminder tone.

Billy never asks for card numbers, PINs or banking passwords. Please don’t type them into notes.

What we receive

Nothing. Billy has no server and no user accounts. We can’t see, read, sell or share your data, because we never have it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about tasks and bills.
  • Camera and photos: only when you take or choose a photo of a bill or attach one to a task.
  • Files and sharing: only for the PDF or text you pick, or share to Billy from another app (for example an emailed bill).

You can turn any permission off in your phone’s settings. Billy still works; that feature just won’t be available.

Reading bills (on your phone)

Bills are read on your phone, including any payment QR codes and “how to pay” instructions. Your photo, PDF or shared text is never uploaded.

The reading software (Tesseract and PDF.js, both open source) is built into Billy. Scanning works without internet, and nothing is downloaded or sent when you scan.

Paying bills

Billy doesn’t make payments or handle money. In India, “Pay” opens your own UPI app with the details filled in; you check and approve the payment there. In Australia, Billy shows the details so you can pay in your bank app. We never see your payments.

If a bill gives a pay-online link (printed or in its QR code), Billy saves it with the bill and opens it in your browser only when you tap Open and confirm. Check it’s your biller’s real website before you pay or sign in. Billy never opens links by itself.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or Google backup, your phone may include Billy’s data in those backups. Apple and Google handle that under their own privacy policies and your account settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Delete tasks in the app, use “Clear” on done tasks, or uninstall Billy to remove everything from the phone. Copies in your phone’s own backups follow your Apple or Google backup settings.

Security

Your data is protected by your phone’s own security. Use a passcode, Face ID or fingerprint lock, and keep your phone’s software up to date.

Children

Billy is a general-audience app and is not made for children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect anyone’s personal information.

Your rights

Privacy laws, including Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, give you rights over your personal information. Because Billy keeps your data only on your phone, you can see, change and delete all of it yourself in the app. If you have a question or complaint, contact us below. In Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If Billy starts collecting or sending anything new, we’ll update this policy and the date below before that version of the app is released.
